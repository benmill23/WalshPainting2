## Services

- [Interior Painting](https://walshpainters.com/services/interior-painting): Walls, ceilings, trim, doors, drywall patching, and color consultation using premium low-VOC paints (Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin Moore).
- [Exterior Painting](https://walshpainters.com/services/exterior-painting): Siding, trim, soffits, decks, and fences — power washed, primed, and finished with weather-resistant coatings for Tennessee climate.
- [Cabinet Refinishing](https://walshpainters.com/services/cabinet-refinishing): Kitchen and bath cabinets refinished with a sprayed, durable enamel finish at a fraction of replacement cost.
- [Commercial Painting](https://walshpainters.com/services/commercial-painting): Offices, retail, restaurants, and property management contracts, scheduled around business hours. Licensed and fully insured.

## Certifications

- [EPA Lead-Safe Certified Firm](https://walshpainters.com/lead-safe-certified): Certified under the EPA Renovation, Repair and Painting (RRP) Rule to safely paint homes built before 1978, following lead-safe work practices that contain lead dust and protect your family.
